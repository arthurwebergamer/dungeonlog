CRT effect in Dungeonlog: an old tube-TV look

2026-10-31

Dungeonlog now has a CRT effect, which gives the app the look of an old tube TV. It goes well with the game's pixel art, and you can turn it on and off whenever you like.

What the effect does

The effect lays thin horizontal lines (scanlines) over the screen, a red, green and blue color mask, a dark vignette around the edges and a slight brightness flicker. Colors get a little more vivid and text gets a faint red and blue fringe, like on a tube screen. For people who prefer less motion, the flicker is turned off when the device has reduced motion enabled.

The effect works over any theme in the app. It does not show on the entry screen (login and intro), so the text stays readable.

How to turn it on or off

After you see the tutorial of the Tasks tab, the app shows a one-time invitation: "Want an old tube-TV look in the app? You can change this anytime in Preferences > CRT effect." You choose Turn on or Not now.

At any time, the "CRT effect" switch in Preferences turns the effect on or off.

The CRT effect is not a Pro feature.
