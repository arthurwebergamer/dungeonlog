Item rarities in Dungeonlog: from Common to Ancestral

2026-10-31

Items in Dungeonlog now have a ladder of eight rarities. It shows in each item's name and color, and it sets how much a piece of equipment helps your hero.

The eight rarities

From most common to rarest: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Superior, Epic, Legendary, Ancestral and Unique. Superior sits between Rare and Epic, and Ancestral sits above Legendary.

Each rarity has its own color, and the item sheet shows the rarity next to the type, like "Rare · Weapon". Superior is pink, so it is not confused with the blue of Rare.

What rarity changes

Weapons, armor, helmets, shields and accessories give a percentage bonus to XP and to coins, and that bonus grows with each step of rarity. A Common weapon gives +5% XP and +3% coins. An Ancestral weapon gives +50% XP and +31% coins. Each type of equipment has its own bonus values.

In the inventory, the equipment panel adds up the bonus from everything you are wearing and shows the total for coins and XP.

How items show up

The higher the rarity, the less often the item appears: Common items are the most frequent, and Ancestral items are the rarest among the ones that drop. There are only a few Ancestral items.
