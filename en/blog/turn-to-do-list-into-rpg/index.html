Turn your to-do list into an RPG

2026-10-31

Dungeonlog is an RPG to-do app: instead of just ticking items off a list, you face one monster per day.

What it is

A monster appears every day. It only falls if you complete all the day's tasks.

How it works

Each task you create has:

  • a name;
  • repetition: today, week, month or year;
  • difficulty: trivial, easy, medium or hard;
  • times per day.

What you get

  • XP and coins;
  • equipment;
  • treasure chests;
  • Fortune, Focus and Vigor attributes;
  • a bestiary of 63 monsters.
