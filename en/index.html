Frequently asked questions

What is Dungeonlog?
It's a to-do app with an RPG theme. You build the day's list, and every finished task earns XP, coins and gear for your hero. Every day there is a monster that is only defeated when all the day's tasks are done.
How does the daily monster work?
A monster appears each day. If you finish all the day's tasks, it falls and you win the day. If a task is left, it escapes and the day doesn't count. It turns your list into a challenge with a start and an end.
Is Dungeonlog free?
There is a free plan and an optional PRO plan. The free plan has limits on some features, such as themes, stats and number of tasks. PRO unlocks everything and costs R$ 17.90 per month, R$ 89.90 per year or R$ 199.90 once (lifetime), in Brazilian reais; prices may vary by country in the store. The annual plan may include a 7-day free trial through Google Play.
Which devices does it work on?
The Android app is available on Google Play. There is no native iPhone app yet.
How is Dungeonlog different from Habitica?
Habitica is an established, free app (with an optional subscription) with parties and group quests. Dungeonlog is simpler and more focused: one monster per day that depends on finishing the day's tasks, a daily shop and pixel-art visuals. If you want community and group quests, Habitica may suit you better. If you want something direct, give Dungeonlog a try.
Can I use it to study?
You can organize study tasks, such as exercises, reviews and readings. It is not a content platform or a subject schedule: it is a task manager with rewards.
Do I need an account?
No. You can continue as a guest and start right away, with no sign-up; in that mode your progress is saved only on this device. If you create an account with Google or email, your progress is kept safe and works on more than one device, and what you did as a guest is carried over to the account. You can delete your account and data at any time.
