Privacy Policy — DungeonLog

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how DungeonLog ("the app", "we") collects, uses and stores user information. By using the app, you agree to the terms described here.

1. Who we are

DungeonLog is a gamified productivity app, developed independently. For questions about this policy or your data, contact us at arthurdungeonlog@gmail.com.

2. What data we collect

Using DungeonLog requires creating an account. We collect:

  • Email — always, whether you sign up with email and password or sign in with Google.
  • Name — only if you sign in with your Google account (we use the first name associated with the account to personalize your experience in the app).
  • Password — if you create an account with email and password, it is processed and securely stored by Firebase Authentication (Google); we do not have access to it in plain text.
  • In-game progress data — tasks you create, level, experience (XP), coins, inventory items, attributes, daily streak, and other information generated by using the app.

We do not collect location data, contacts, camera, microphone, or financial information (the app does not process real payments at this time).

3. How we use your data

We use the information we collect exclusively to:

  • Authenticate your account and allow sign-in on different devices;
  • Sync and keep your game progress saved in the cloud;
  • Display your name inside the app (when available via Google).

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not perform behavioral analytics, and we do not share, sell or rent your information to third parties for commercial purposes.

4. Where your data is stored

Your account and progress data are stored on Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), Google's cloud infrastructure service. In addition, a copy of your progress data is saved locally on your device, so the app keeps working even without an internet connection.

5. Sharing with third parties

We use the following third-party services, strictly for the app's technical operation:

  • Google Firebase — account authentication and storage/sync of progress data.
  • Google Fonts — loads the fonts used in the app's interface (this may expose your device's IP address to Google's servers at load time, a standard web practice).

We do not use analytics tools (such as Google Analytics), we do not show ads, and we do not integrate payment processors at this time.

6. Notifications

The app has technical infrastructure for sending push notifications (via Firebase Cloud Messaging), but this feature is not active in the current version. If it is enabled in the future, this policy will be updated to reflect the collection of the device's notification token.

7. Your rights

Under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD) and other applicable regulations, you have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you;
  • Correct inaccurate information (name, email);
  • Delete your progress data — available directly in the app, under Settings > Delete data. This action removes your game data both from the device and from Firebase;
  • Delete your account entirely (including your login credentials) — send a request to arthurdungeonlog@gmail.com, and we will complete the deletion within 15 business days.

8. Security

We employ the standard security practices provided by Firebase (Google), including secure authentication and encrypted communication (HTTPS) between the app and its servers.

9. Children

DungeonLog is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children in that age group. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can remove it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Significant changes will be communicated within the app. The date at the top of this document reflects the most recent version.

11. Contact

Questions, data access or deletion requests: arthurdungeonlog@gmail.com
