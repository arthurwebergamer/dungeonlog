Why this isn't just "another Habitica"

Gamified to-do apps stack XP on top of a list — the list is still a list, it just got a little score attached. DungeonLog flips that: there's an active opponent. Leave tasks unfinished and the monster doesn't disappear — it stays there, watching, until the day ends.
